# Boards, Epics, and Iterations

Read when the team wants a board, epics, or iterations rather than
plain issues and milestones.

1. Verify tier first: locate the boards, epics, and iterations topics
   through the llms.txt index and read each page's tier badge on the
   target instance's version. Epics and iterations are paid-tier;
   multiple boards and scoped labels may be too. What the tier lacks
   falls back to labels plus milestones — design that fallback before
   proposing the paid feature.
2. A board is a standing commitment: agree who grooms it and on what
   rhythm before creating it. A board nobody grooms misleads more than
   no board.
3. Start from the smallest structure that answers the team's actual
   question ("what is in flight?", "what is next?") — one board whose
   lists mirror how work really moves, built on label or milestone
   scopes fetched from the current docs.
4. Keep issues the source of truth: boards and epics arrange them, they
   never replace them.
5. Record in the AGENTS.md deposit: the board's location, what each
   list means, who grooms it, and — when epics are in play — what an
   epic groups that a milestone does not.
